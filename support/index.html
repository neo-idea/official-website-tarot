Frequently asked

Where are my readings stored?
All readings stay on your device. They are not uploaded to a server.

Can I restore Premium?
Yes. Open the Premium sheet and choose Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID.

Can I delete my history?
Yes. Use the history page in the app to remove individual readings, or reset app data from iOS Settings.

Does the app work offline?
The full card library and readings work offline. Ads require a network connection.

For account-independent issues, reinstalling the app will not transfer your local reading history. Export or keep anything you need before deleting the app.
